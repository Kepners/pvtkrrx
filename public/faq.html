Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Straight answers for PVTKRRX, SportsMeta, and Clockrr.

PVTKRRX is a private tracker and seedbox bridge for Stremio. It does not host your media, does not replace your stack, and does not work like debrid.

What is PVTKRRX?
PVTKRRX is a Stremio addon system for private tracker and seedbox operators. It connects your Prowlarr or Torznab search setup and qBittorrent library to Stremio.
Does PVTKRRX host media?
No. PVTKRRX is not a third-party media host. Playback comes from your Windows host, your seedbox, or your own public HTTPS file/playback endpoint depending on the route.
Is PVTKRRX a debrid service?
No. PVTKRRX is not debrid and does not replace private trackers. The point is control: your hardware, your trackers, your configured infrastructure.
What do I need before setup?
You need Stremio, Prowlarr or another Torznab-compatible search service, qBittorrent with WebUI enabled, and a host that matches your playback route.
Which route should I use first?
Use PC Local on the Windows PC running PVTKRRX. Use LAN Bridge for other home devices on the same Stremio account. Use Remote Seedbox when playback needs to work through public HTTPS endpoints away from home.
Where should I enter private Prowlarr and qBittorrent details?
Enter them on the runtime that owns playback. For Windows local use, open http://127.0.0.1:7000/configure on that PC. For self-hosted server use, open https://your-domain/configure. The public website is guide-only.
Where do sports fit?
Sports is a first-class PVTKRRX catalog alongside movies, TV, and library rows. PVTKRRX shows sports releases that your trackers already carry. It is not a live sports service.
What is SportsMeta?
SportsMeta is the separate sports identity and artwork service at sportsmeta.pvtkrrx.cc. It owns canonical sportsmeta: IDs, public SVG-first artwork routes, member artwork routes, and SportsMeta billing. PVTKRRX consumes SportsMeta; it does not become SportsMeta.
Does SportsMeta payment change PVTKRRX streaming?
No. PVTKRRX streaming stays free on the tested surfaces. SportsMeta Plus and Pro are about SportsMeta member artwork and member routes, not a paid PVTKRRX stream gate.
What is Clockrr?
Clockrr is a separate Stremio subtitle addon at clockrr.pvtkrrx.cc. It adds a configurable clock overlay using generated WebVTT subtitle cues for movies and series. It does not provide catalogs or streams.
