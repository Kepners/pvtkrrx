Clear notes on PVTKRRX, SportsMeta, and Clockrr.

This page keeps crawlable product updates in one place: route truth, SportsMeta boundaries, Clockrr status, and setup guidance for private tracker and seedbox operators using Stremio.

April 25, 2026 / Clockrr

Clockrr joins the PVTKRRX.CC family surface

Clockrr is now linked from the public PVTKRRX.CC site, but it remains a separate Stremio subtitle addon. It exposes the subtitles resource for movies and series, adds a configurable clock through WebVTT cues, and keeps its own manifest at clockrr.pvtkrrx.cc.

That separation matters. PVTKRRX is still the private tracker and seedbox bridge. Clockrr does not provide catalogs, metadata, streams, or media hosting.

April 25, 2026 / SportsMeta

SportsMeta status: free streams, separate artwork membership

PVTKRRX streams remain free on the tested route surfaces. SportsMeta is the companion identity and artwork service on its own hostname. Public SportsMeta keeps canonical sportsmeta: IDs and SVG-first artwork available, while SportsMeta Plus and Pro belong to the member install and artwork routes.

PVTKRRX consumes SportsMeta for sports identity and artwork, then serves client-safe PVTKRRX artwork URLs where the stream addon needs them. Payment does not turn PVTKRRX into a hosted media provider or a debrid service.

April 25, 2026 / Setup

Which PVTKRRX route should you start with?

Start with PC Local on the Windows machine running PVTKRRX. Use LAN Bridge for your TV, phone, and other home devices on the same Stremio account. Use Remote Seedbox when playback needs public HTTPS endpoints away from home.

The public site is guide-only. Private Prowlarr and qBittorrent details belong on the runtime that owns playback: your Windows host or your own self-host server origin.
