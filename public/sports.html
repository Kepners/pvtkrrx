What You Get

Tracker-first sports, presented properly

The content source is still your own tracker stack. PVTKRRX handles the Stremio addon surface, route choice, stream attachment, and sports-specific catalog shape.

Sports Catalog

Sports appears as a first-class catalog alongside movies, TV, and library. Browse by sport family and narrow results inside Stremio.

SportsMeta Identity

Canonical sportsmeta: IDs and artwork come from SportsMeta, the companion identity and artwork service in the same product stack.

Clean Separation

Sports-only indexers stay out of movie and TV search paths, so match packs, race weekends, and fight cards do not pollute normal discovery.

Flow

How sports moves through the stack

1
You configure

Point PVTKRRX at Prowlarr and qBittorrent, then choose PC Local, LAN Bridge, or Remote Seedbox.

2
Trackers answer

Sports releases come from the trackers you already have access to through your own Prowlarr setup.

3
SportsMeta resolves

Where possible, sports rows attach to stable SportsMeta event identities and artwork surfaces.

4
Stremio plays

Playback still follows your selected route. PVTKRRX does not host the media or replace your infrastructure.

Boundary

PVTKRRX is the stream addon. It searches your tracker setup and attaches playable streams. SportsMeta is the companion sports identity and artwork service. It owns canonical sports metadata and artwork routes on its own hostname. PVTKRRX consumes the public SportsMeta surface, but PVTKRRX itself is not a live sports service, debrid service, media host, or paid artwork entitlement tier.

Checkout

Upgrade the artwork, not the stream addon

Sports Posters is optional. PVTKRRX keeps working without it, and payment does not change where playback comes from. It only unlocks SportsMeta member artwork for sports posters, backgrounds, and logos.
