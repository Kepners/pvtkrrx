What You Get

Catalog, artwork, and metadata

Sports Catalog

Sports appears as a top-level catalog in Stremio. Browse, filter by genre, and search — the same way you use movies and TV. All results come from your own trackers.

Artwork Enrichment

Sports metadata carries posters, landscape art, and logos via TheSportsDB. The Stremio surface looks and feels like a real catalog, not a list of filenames.

Contamination Filter

Sports indexers are automatically excluded from movie and TV searches. Your film results stay clean — sports content only appears in the sports catalog.

One Addon Family

Sports, movies, TV, and your downloaded library all live in one addon. Already-downloaded files sit alongside new releases. No separate install required.

How It Works

Your trackers, your sports

PVTKRRX searches your Prowlarr indexers for sports releases and presents them in Stremio with enriched metadata. The content comes from the trackers you already have access to — PVTKRRX is the bridge, not the source.

Sports support depends on your tracker catalogue. PVTKRRX is not a live sports service or a separate content provider. It surfaces what your trackers carry.
