Flow

How sports moves through the stack

1
You configure

Point PVTKRRX at Prowlarr and qBittorrent, then choose PC Local, LAN Bridge, or Remote Seedbox.

2
Trackers answer

Sports releases come from the trackers you already have access to through your own Prowlarr setup.

3
SportsMeta resolves

Where possible, sports rows attach to stable SportsMeta event identities. SportsMeta-owned artwork remains the enrichment source, and PVTKRRX transports client-safe artwork on its own route.

4
Stremio plays

Playback still follows your selected route. PVTKRRX does not host the media or replace your infrastructure.

Boundary

PVTKRRX is the stream addon. It searches your tracker setup and attaches playable streams. SportsMeta is the companion sports identity and artwork service. It owns canonical sports metadata and artwork routes on its own hostname. PVTKRRX consumes the public SportsMeta surface and can proxy client-safe artwork, but PVTKRRX itself is not a live sports service, debrid service, media host, or paid artwork entitlement tier.
